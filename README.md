# Infinity-Business-Solutions

A modern, responsive corporate website built using **HTML, CSS, and JavaScript**, designed to showcase business services, company information, and interactive client inquiry experiences.

## 🌐 Project Overview

Infinity Business Solutions is a professional business website designed with a modern UI, responsive layout, smooth animations, and interactive elements. It provides visitors with an overview of the company, its services, and ways to connect with the business.

The website focuses on delivering a clean user experience across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

## ✨ Features

- **Modern Landing Page** – Professional hero section with company branding and animated text.
- **About Section** – Company overview, mission, and business introduction.
- **Services Section** – Interactive cards showcasing various business services.
- **Service Inquiry Modal** – Popup form for requesting specific services.
- **Why Choose Us** – Highlights the company's strengths and advantages.
- **Contact Section** – Contact information and inquiry form.
- **Responsive Navigation** – Mobile-friendly navigation menu.
- **Smooth Scrolling** – Smooth navigation between sections.
- **Scroll Animations** – Reveal animations as elements enter the viewport.
- **Hover Effects** – Interactive animations for buttons, cards, and navigation links.
- **Back-to-Top Button** – Quickly return to the top of the page.
